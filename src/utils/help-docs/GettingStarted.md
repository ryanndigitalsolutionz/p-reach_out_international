
# Getting Started with P-REACH OUT

Welcome to P-REACH OUT International!

We're glad to have you here. Our platform brings together personal wellbeing resources, community participation, educational materials and opportunities to connect with others.

Whether you're here to explore our resources, participate in community activities or keep in touch with our team, this guide will help you get started.

## 1. Create Your Member Account

To access the P-REACH OUT member space, you'll first need an account.

1. Visit the [P-REACH OUT homepage](/).
2. Select **Sign Up** to create a new account. Returning members can select **Log In**.
3. During registration, enter your date of birth to confirm that you meet the minimum age requirement of **18 years**.
4. Continue with your email address.
5. Check your email for the verification code sent to you.
6. Enter the code to verify your email address and continue setting up your account.

**Important:** Your date of birth can only be submitted once during registration. Make sure the information is correct before proceeding. If you believe you've entered an incorrect date or were incorrectly marked as ineligible, contact the P-REACH OUT team for assistance.

Never share your email verification code with anyone.

## 2. Customize Your Profile (Optional)

Once you've created your account, you can personalize your member profile.

Visit **My Profile** under your account settings to:

- Add a profile picture, where profile image uploads are available.
- Write a short biography describing yourself.
- Introduce yourself to other members through your profile.

You can also explore the Help Docs and Policies sections to familiarize yourself with the platform, its features and the rules governing participation.

## 3. Explore Your Member Dashboard

Your dashboard is your starting point for accessing the member features.

Here are a few things you can explore:

### Check In

Use the Check-In feature regularly, such as once a week, to reflect on your wellbeing and follow your personal progress.

Your check-in information is personal to you.

### Groups

Explore the available community groups.

Depending on the groups and permissions available, you can join an existing group or create one where group creation is enabled.

Groups provide a space for members to communicate, share information and participate in community conversations.

### Journal

Use your personal Journal as a diary.

Write down your thoughts, reflections, experiences and personal goals. Take the time you need to express yourself.

### People Hub

Connect with other members through People Hub.

Explore community posts and participate in conversations as the available features permit.

### P-R News

Keep up with P-REACH OUT International through P-R News.

Discover organizational information, mission activities, programmes and updates about the community's work.

## 4. Find More Help

You don't have to figure everything out alone.

Explore the Help Docs whenever you need guidance on using a feature or understanding how the platform works.

You can also visit the Policies section to understand your responsibilities, member rights and the rules governing participation.

For questions, feedback or assistance, use the available contact and feedback channels to reach the P-REACH OUT team.
