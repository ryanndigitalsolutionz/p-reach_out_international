
# Data Privacy at P-REACH OUT

Your privacy matters.

P-REACH OUT International provides a platform where members can access resources, participate in community activities and interact with the organization. We encourage everyone to understand how their personal information should be handled and how to protect it while using the platform.

This guide explains practical privacy considerations when using P-REACH OUT.

## 1. Your Account Information

Your member account may contain information used to identify and manage your account, including:

- Your name and email address.
- Your date of birth, provided during registration.
- Your profile information and biography.
- Other information you choose to provide when using platform features.

Keep your account information accurate and avoid sharing details that you do not want other people to know.

Never disclose your password or email verification codes to another person.

## 2. Your Profile and Community Activity

Your profile helps other members become familiar with you.

Before publishing information through your profile, Groups or People Hub, consider whether it is appropriate for other members to see it.

Avoid posting:

- Passwords and account verification codes.
- Identity-document numbers or photographs.
- Private contact information that you do not wish to share.
- Personal information belonging to another person without their permission.
- Sensitive personal or counselling information.

Remember that information shared in a community space may be seen, copied or shared by other participants.

## 3. Check-In and Personal Wellbeing Information

The Check-In feature gives members a space to reflect on their wellbeing and follow their personal progress.

Your check-in answers and assessment details are private to you. They are not displayed to other members or included in community activity.

Be thoughtful about sharing personal wellbeing information elsewhere on the platform.

For information about how personal records are handled and who may access them, consult the [Privacy Policy](/policies/privacy).

## 4. Your Journal

Your Journal is intended for personal writing and reflection.

Use it to document your thoughts, experiences and goals. Avoid copying private information belonging to other people into your entries without permission.

For questions about how journal entries are stored, accessed or retained, refer to the Privacy Policy.

## 5. Groups and Messages

Groups provide a space for community communication.

Keep in mind that group conversations are shared with the participants who have access to that group.

Before sending a message, image or other attachment:

- Consider whether the information should be shared with the whole group.
- Respect the privacy of other participants.
- Do not distribute another member's private messages without appropriate permission.
- Avoid sharing confidential counselling information in ordinary group conversations.

Community participation should always respect the dignity and privacy of others.

## 6. Account and Device Security

You can help protect your account by following these practices:

- Use a strong, unique password.
- Do not share your account credentials.
- Keep access to your email account secure.
- Sign out when using a shared or public device.
- Avoid opening suspicious links or attachments.
- Contact the P-REACH OUT team if you suspect unauthorized access to your account.

If you receive a security notification you do not recognize, take it seriously and review your account.

## 7. Your Privacy Questions and Requests

You may contact the P-REACH OUT team when you have concerns or questions about your personal information.

Examples include:

- Requesting clarification about information associated with your account.
- Reporting a suspected privacy or security incident.
- Asking about access to or correction of your personal information.
- Raising concerns about how information is being handled.
- Making a request concerning the deletion or retention of personal information.

Use the available contact and feedback channels to reach the organization.

The team may need to verify your identity before responding to certain account or privacy requests.

## 8. Read the Privacy Policy

This article provides practical guidance. It does not replace the organization's formal Privacy Policy.

The Privacy Policy explains how P-REACH OUT handles personal information, including its purposes for collecting information, applicable legal grounds, sharing arrangements, retention practices and the rights available to individuals.

Please read the [Privacy Policy](/policies/privacy) for the formal details.

---

**Your privacy deserves respect. Protect your information, respect the information of others and contact P-REACH OUT whenever you need clarification.**
